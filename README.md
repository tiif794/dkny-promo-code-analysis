## The restrictions that quietly kill most discounts

DKNY's own promotion terms are stricter than the coupon sites let on, and they explain nearly every "why didn't my code work" complaint. These rules come straight from the brand's promotions page:

- **Final sale items are excluded** from all promo codes, along with select new arrivals.
- **Codes cannot be combined.** One code per order, full stop — including welcome codes, which explicitly cannot be stacked with other promo codes.
- **Site only.** Promotions apply to the online store and are not valid in physical retail locations. In-store and online prices can differ, so the discount you saw advertised may not match your local store.
- **Gift cards don't qualify**, codes can't be applied to previous purchases, and nothing can be redeemed for cash.
- DKNY reserves the right to end any promotion at any time, and product availability is not guaranteed.

That "select new arrivals" exclusion is the sneaky one. A 20% code that works on one handbag in the same collection may bounce on another because it's flagged as a new arrival. Some categories — watches come up repeatedly in tracker notes — are frequently excluded from the welcome discount entirely. There's no published exclusion list, so the only reliable test is entering the code and watching whether the total changes.

## FAQ

No. DKNY's terms explicitly prohibit combining promo codes, and welcome codes get the same restriction. One code per order.

Because it's not supposed to. Codes exclude final sale items and most markdowns — the system rejects them automatically. Save the code for full-price pieces.

Sporadically. Based on one tracker's checkout data, DKNY had at least one working code in roughly 15 out of the past 46 weeks — about a third of the time. The sale section is the dependable discount channel; codes are the bonus.

**Do DKNY promotions work in physical stores?**
Not the online codes. DKNY's terms state promotions are site-only and that in-store and online prices may differ.
